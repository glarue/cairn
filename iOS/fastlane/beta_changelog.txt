0.4.3 (build 145) — tidier quarantine queue, and a couple of countdown fixes.

Big deletions no longer bury the rest. When a bunch of photos are queued to expire on the same day (a bulk delete), they now stack into a single "N photos" card in Pending Review instead of a long run of individual rows. Tap it to expand and review or act on them one by one; the trash/exclude/dismiss chips on the card act on the whole batch. Smaller same-day sets still show as individual cards.

Two countdown fixes:
- The quarantine list no longer reshuffles its order when you reopen the app — it now sorts soonest-to-trash first, consistently.
- The Status "next in N days" banner now matches the countdown shown on the photo cards in the drill-down (they could disagree by a day before).

To test: if you have items in quarantine, reopen the app a few times and confirm the order stays put. Compare the "next in N days" on the Status card to the soonest item inside Pending Review — they should match. If you have 5+ photos deleted on the same day, confirm they appear as one stacked card that expands.
