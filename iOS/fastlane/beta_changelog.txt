0.4.3 (build 147) — automatic trashing (opt-in), and a quarantine fix.

Big one this build: cairn can now move deletions to Immich's Trash automatically, without a manual "Trash all" tap. This is OPT-IN and gated by your safety limits.

- Please check Settings → Safety & limits → Deletion strictness. If you were on Balanced or Autonomous, this build resets you to Cautious (manual) one time — because no previous version ever auto-deleted, we don't want to switch anyone into automatic deletion silently. Re-select Balanced or Autonomous to turn auto-trashing on.
- Cautious (the new default): nothing is trashed automatically — deletions wait as "Ready to trash" for you to confirm.
- Balanced: past-quarantine deletions are trashed automatically on the next sync.
- Autonomous: deletions are trashed automatically with no quarantine wait.
- In all cases it's gated by your percent + count safety limits: a run that would exceed them (or a first sync) is NOT auto-deleted — it's left for you to review. Everything goes to Immich's Trash (30-day recovery), and auto-trash runs on background refresh too.

Also fixed: trashing the "Ready to trash" list no longer makes the quarantine banner disappear until the next sync — it now stays accurate immediately.

To test: confirm your strictness is Cautious after updating. Then, if you want auto-trash, set Balanced or Autonomous, delete a few photos on your phone, and after the quarantine window (or immediately, for Autonomous) confirm they move to Immich's Trash on the next sync without a tap. Try a large deletion to confirm the safety limit still holds it back for manual review.
